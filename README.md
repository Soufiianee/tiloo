# Store Luxe Chiffrage (prototype)

A quoting tool for [storeluxe.ma](https://www.storeluxe.ma/). A salesperson picks a product, enters dimensions and options, and gets a safe selling price in a few seconds, with a ready-to-paste WhatsApp message.

```
app/devis.html   page (source; published as-is as an Artifact)
docs/etude-prix.md  supplier price study (sources, today's prices, maximums)
app/index.html   standalone version generated by `npm run build` (open locally or host it)
app/catalog.js   products, their parameters, quantity takeoff rules, example supplier prices
app/engine.js    pricing engine (pure functions, tested)
app/app.js       interface
tests/           `npm test`
```

Open `app/index.html` in a browser. Nothing to install, and no server needed.

---

## 1. What the research found

### Store Luxe (Agadir, since 1999)

The site publishes **no prices**. Every product page ends with "devis après relevé" (quote after site survey) plus a WhatsApp link. Each page also lists the factors that drive the price, and those factors became the tool's parameters:

| Product | Price drivers listed on the site → tool parameters |
|---|---|
| Bioclimatic pergola | surface, bays, wall-mounted / freestanding, motorisation, sensors, lighting, side ZIP screens, colour, site access |
| Fixed aluminium pergola | surface, roofing (polycarbonate / glass / panel), number of posts, wall-mounted / freestanding, lighting, screens |
| Veranda | surface, height, glazing (clear / solar control / laminated), roof, openings, rooftop terrace |
| Shade sail | fabric area, fabric type (breathable / waterproof), number of masts and footings, anchor points |
| Carport | single / double, span, roofing, ground (concrete slab / footings), lighting |
| Awning | width, projection, monobloc / semi-cassette / full cassette, fabric, motorisation, wall type |
| ZIP screen | width × height, fabric (screen / blackout), motorisation, mounting on pergola or façade |
| Roller blind, day & night blind, Venetian blind | dimensions, fabric / slats, cassette, side guides, motorisation, quantity |
| Insect screen | dimensions, system (fixed / sliding / roll-up / pleated), powder coating |

Brands they install (*Marques* page): **Sauleda** and **Serge Ferrari** fabrics, **Gaviota, Llaza, Siplan** systems, **Somfy** motors. These components are imported and priced in euros, which matters for the method below.

### Perlago (Casablanca), the competitor's configurator

This is PrestaShop with **fixed option combinations**. I queried the shop's endpoint to get the price of each one (prices TTC, before installation):

| ATLAS bioclimatic pergola | TTC price |
|---|---|
| 5 × 3 m (15 m²) | 63,163 MAD (4,211/m²) |
| 5 × 4 m (20 m²) | 77,050 MAD (3,853/m²) |
| 6 × 4 m (24 m²) | 91,685 MAD (3,820/m²) |
| + 8 spots | +5,008 MAD |
| + 16 m white LED / RGB LED | +6,548 / +8,346 MAD |
| Installation (separate product) | 5,000 MAD |
| 9 RAL colours | no price difference |

What this shows:
- Their m² price **falls** as size grows, so there is a fixed part plus a part proportional to area.
- The options only work in 5 × 3. In larger sizes the price ignores them, which is a bug in their configurator.
- It is a frozen grid with no supplier cost or site behind it. It is good for marketing, but it can't follow the prices of aluminium or the euro.

## 2. The method: cost-plus pricing at the **ceiling** price

Your intuition is right: quote from the highest price, not the latest one. To keep it competitive, the tool frames it like this:

```
Ceiling cost of an item = highest price over a rolling window (180 d) × (1 + buffer 2 %)
    aluminium profile    = kg/m × ceiling aluminium rate + fabrication
    imported item (€)    = ceiling € price × ceiling EUR/MAD rate × (1 + landed costs 25 %)

Product cost       = Σ materials × (1 + offcuts) + Σ equipment + Σ labour × access factor
Price excl. VAT    = Product cost ÷ (1 − target margin), rounded up to the next 50 MAD
Quote              = Σ prices + travel (+ nights away) − discount;  incl. VAT = excl. VAT × 1.20
```

How it addresses the problem:

1. **Two indices instead of hundreds of prices.** Most of the daily movement comes from **aluminium (MAD/kg)** and the **EUR/MAD rate** (Somfy, Sauleda, Ferrari, Gaviota). Update those two numbers and every profile and imported item follows. The other items get updated whenever a supplier announces a new price.
2. **The ceiling protects the margin.** If aluminium rises between the quote and the supplier order, you are already covered. When it falls, the gap becomes extra margin. Each quote shows this as "marge si achat aujourd'hui" (margin if you buy today) next to the target margin.
3. **Three guardrails keep you from becoming too expensive:**
   - a **rolling window**, so a peak from a year ago doesn't follow you forever (configurable: window / all history / latest price);
   - a **quote validity period** (15 days), which limits how long you carry the risk;
   - a **"to check" alert** on any price not updated for more than 30 days.
4. **Takeoff by component.** Each product turns its parameters into quantities: m² of slats, m of beams, number of posts, m² of fabric, motors… Adding an option means adding a line, with no price table to maintain for each combination (unlike Perlago).

Calibration: at the recommended level, using the study prices, the tool gives 69,240 / 82,020 / 93,000 MAD incl. VAT (installation included) for the three ATLAS sizes. Perlago is at 68,163 / 82,050 / 96,685 with installation. The test `tests/engine.test.mjs` checks the ±10 % gap.

## 2b. Three price levels (since v2)

Every quote shows three prices, chosen from the study (`docs/etude-prix.md`):

- **Floor (plancher)**: the latest price. The minimum that holds the target margin if you buy today.
- **Recommended (conseillé, default)**: the highest price of the last 12 months + 2 %.
- **Prudent**: the highest price ever recorded or found (e.g. aluminium at its March 2022 record) + 2 %.

The level can be chosen per quote line. The aluminium index can be recalculated from the LME price: (LME + premium + extrusion & coating) × USD/MAD.

## 2c. Team mode (since v3)

Opened from claude.ai, the page uses a database shared by everyone the artifact is shared with:

- **Prices**: one shared price list. Each new price records who entered it and when ("Dernières mises à jour" in *Prix & sources*).
- **Settings** (margins, policy, zones, conditions): shared, editable by the page's editors only.
- **Quotes**: every quote is saved and visible to the team, with statuses draft → sent → accepted / lost (with reason) → completed.
- **Locked prices**: once a quote is sent, its client prices no longer change. If suppliers have raised prices since, the quote shows the real margin.
- **Job review**: on a completed quote, enter the real cost and real installation days. The list shows the average gap between estimate and actual, plus the signing rate.

Without claude.ai (for example `app/index.html` opened locally), the tool runs in **local mode**: same features, data stored in the browser. Prices and quotes entered locally can then be shared with the team in one click.

## 3. Using the tool

- **Configurer** (configure): city, access, client → product → dimensions/options. A dimensioned drawing (top view, profile or front view) updates live. The price excl. and incl. VAT shows instantly, with the bar for cost today / ceiling cushion / margin, technical alerts, and the internal cost breakdown. "Ajouter au devis" adds the line to the quote. A quote can combine products (pergola + ZIP + awning). "Copier pour WhatsApp" copies the message to send.
- **Devis** (quote): a client-ready document (number, validity, lines, totals, conditions), the price level per line, "Modifier" to reopen a line, WhatsApp message or plain text to copy.
- **Prix & sources** (prices & sources): the aluminium and EUR/MAD indices (today / 12 months / maximum), an LME calculator, then every item with its source and history.
- **Étude de marché** (market study): the research summary with its sources.
- **Réglages** (settings): pricing policy, buffer, landed costs, VAT, rounding, validity, **target margin per product**, labour factors, travel zones. Backup by copy/paste (JSON) to share with the team.

## 4. Limits of this prototype and next steps

- **Starting prices come from the study (public prices and market ranges), not from your suppliers.** Before any real quote, enter your purchase prices (the two indices first, then the items marked "à vérifier").
- The takeoff rules (posts every 4 m, slats in 6 m modules, labour days…) are reasonable defaults. They should be adjusted on 5 or 6 real past jobs, comparing actual cost against what the tool computes.
- Possible later additions: PDF quote with logo, a simplified public configurator for the site (showing a range "from X to Y MAD"), and history of sent quotes.
